MikroMeet is self-hosted WebRTC meetings with lightweight signaling, rooms, chat, and moderation. It is for teams that want control more than a global managed meeting network.

It is not meant to be better than every alternative. It is meant to be a better fit when ownership, simplicity, and a smaller operational surface matter.

## Quick Comparison

| Area | MikroMeet | Common alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting | Self-hosted signaling and static frontend | Managed SaaS or self-hostable stacks |
| Meetings | Rooms, media controls, chat, reactions | Often broader calendar and workplace suites |
| Scale | Best for small controlled meetings | Managed platforms handle global scale better |
| Ownership | You control deployment and data path choices | Provider controls most infrastructure |
| Best fit | Owned lightweight meetings | Large webinars, PSTN, enterprise conferencing |

## Where MikroMeet Fits

- You want meetings on infrastructure you control.
- You need lightweight rooms without a workplace suite.
- You are comfortable operating the deployment.

## When Another Tool May Be Better

- You need dial-in numbers, recordings, webinars, or global TURN capacity.
- You need enterprise compliance and managed support.
- You host very large or highly distributed calls.

## Bottom Line

Choose MikroMeet when you want a focused tool you can understand, run, and keep. Choose a larger platform when you need its scale, ecosystem, managed operations, or specialized workflows.
